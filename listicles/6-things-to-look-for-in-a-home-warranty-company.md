# 6 Things to Look for in a Home Warranty Company

Your home’s systems and appliances work hard every day. Heating and cooling systems keep your home comfortable, while water heaters, kitchen appliances, plumbing systems, and electrical components all play an important role in keeping your household running smoothly.

When one of these essential items unexpectedly breaks down, repair or replacement costs can add stress quickly. That’s why many homeowners ask: **What should I look for in a home warranty company?**

The best home warranty company is not necessarily the same for every homeowner. Your home, budget, appliances, systems, and repair preferences all matter. As you compare home warranty providers, look for a company that offers clear coverage, helpful support, flexible options, and a straightforward process for handling covered breakdowns.

Here are six important factors to consider when choosing a home warranty company.

## 1. Contractor Choice

One of the most important questions to ask when comparing home warranty companies is: **Who gets to choose the contractor?**

Some home warranty providers assign contractors from their own networks. While that approach may work for some homeowners, others prefer the flexibility to work with a local professional they already know and trust.

America’s Preferred Home Warranty (APHW) gives homeowners more control by allowing customers to choose their own licensed contractor. After starting a claim, homeowners can select a licensed contractor to diagnose the problem and work with APHW to determine coverage before covered repairs begin.

This can be particularly valuable if you already have an HVAC technician, plumber, electrician, or appliance repair professional you trust. When something goes wrong in your home, having more control over who performs the work can help make the repair process feel more manageable.

## 2. Price and Coverage

Price is naturally an important consideration, but the cheapest home warranty is not always the one that provides the best value.

When comparing plans, look closely at what is included, what is excluded, applicable deductibles or service fees, coverage limits, and available optional coverage. Consider the systems and appliances you rely on most and whether the plan provides the protection that matters to your household.

It is also important to remember that a home warranty is different from homeowners insurance and does not cover every potential home repair. Coverage is determined by the warranty agreement and may include specific limitations, exclusions, deductibles, and claim requirements.

APHW offers coverage for a variety of major systems and appliances throughout the home, including heating and air conditioning systems, water heaters, electrical systems, plumbing, kitchen appliances, washers and dryers, and other eligible items. Optional coverage is also available for homeowners who want additional protection.

Before choosing any home warranty company, review the coverage details carefully so you understand what you are purchasing.

## 3. Customer Service

When something breaks in your home, you do not want to spend hours wondering who to contact or what to do next.

That makes customer support another important factor when evaluating home warranty companies. Problems do not always happen during normal business hours. An HVAC breakdown, water heater problem, plumbing issue, or appliance failure can happen at any time.

APHW provides 24/7 customer support to help homeowners start claims, understand the process, and get answers when they need assistance.

When comparing providers, look beyond the price of the plan. Consider how easy the company is to contact, when assistance is available, and whether you can get clear answers about claims and coverage.

A home warranty should help make an unexpected covered repair more manageable—not make the process more confusing.

## 4. Claims Process

A straightforward claims process can make a significant difference when you are already dealing with a broken system or appliance.

Before selecting a home warranty provider, make sure you understand:

- How do you start a claim?
- Can claims be submitted online?
- Can you speak with someone if you need help?
- When is authorization required?
- Who contacts the contractor?
- What happens after the problem is diagnosed?

APHW allows homeowners to start a claim online or by phone. After receiving a claim number, the homeowner can select a licensed contractor to diagnose the issue. The contractor then works with APHW to determine applicable coverage before covered repair work begins.

Regardless of which provider you choose, understand the claims process before you need to use it. When something goes wrong, knowing the next step can help reduce unnecessary stress and delays.

## 5. Flexibility for Your Home

No two homes are exactly alike.

A newer home may have different coverage priorities than a house with systems and appliances that have been in service for many years. Some homeowners may be particularly concerned about their heating and air conditioning systems, while others may want additional protection for appliances, plumbing, electrical systems, or other property features.

That is why flexibility matters when comparing home warranty companies.

Look for coverage that can accommodate the needs of your particular home rather than selecting a plan based solely on price. Depending on the provider, optional coverage may allow you to expand protection for additional items that are important to you.

APHW offers standard home warranty coverage along with optional add-ons that can provide additional protection for eligible items beyond its basic coverage.

Consider the age and condition of your home’s major systems and appliances, your budget, and which unexpected repairs would have the greatest financial impact when comparing your options.

## 6. A Company That Makes Coverage Clear

Home warranty coverage should be understandable.

Before purchasing a plan, take time to review the agreement and ask questions about anything that is unclear. Make sure you understand what may be covered, applicable coverage limits, exclusions, deductibles, and the steps required when submitting a claim.

A trustworthy home warranty provider should explain that coverage applies according to the terms of the agreement rather than suggesting that every problem or repair will automatically be covered.

You should also understand the difference between an item being listed as part of a home warranty plan and every possible problem with that item being covered. Specific components, causes of failure, limits, and exclusions may apply.

Clear expectations upfront can help you make a more informed decision and better understand how to use your home warranty when you need it.
