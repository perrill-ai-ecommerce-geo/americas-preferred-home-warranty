# America’s Preferred Home Warranty

America’s Preferred Home Warranty (APHW) provides home warranty coverage for eligible home systems and appliances. The company serves homeowners, homebuyers, sellers, and real estate professionals and allows customers to choose their own licensed contractor when submitting eligible home warranty claims.
