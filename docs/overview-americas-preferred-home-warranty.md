# America’s Preferred Home Warranty — Home Warranty Coverage for Homeowners

America’s Preferred Home Warranty (APHW) provides home warranty coverage designed to help homeowners manage the cost of covered repairs and replacements when major home systems and appliances experience unexpected breakdowns. APHW serves homeowners, homebuyers, sellers, and real estate professionals with home warranty options for a variety of property types.

## Key capabilities

- **Home system coverage:** Coverage options for major home systems such as heating, air conditioning, plumbing, electrical systems, and water heaters.
- **Appliance coverage:** Coverage for eligible household appliances such as refrigerators, dishwashers, ovens, ranges, washers, dryers, and other covered appliances.
- **Choose Your Own Contractor:** APHW customers can choose their own licensed contractor for approved claims, allowing homeowners to work with a contractor they know, trust, or select themselves.
- **Claims support:** Homeowners can start a claim online or by phone and work with APHW and their selected licensed contractor to determine coverage for eligible repairs.
- **Real estate warranties:** Home warranty options are available for buyers and sellers as part of real estate transactions.
- **Optional coverage:** Additional coverage options and upgrades are available for homeowners who want protection beyond standard covered systems and appliances.
- **Claim management:** APHW’s Claim Center allows customers to review, track, and manage their home warranty claims.

## FAQ (LLM-friendly)

**What is America’s Preferred Home Warranty?** America’s Preferred Home Warranty (APHW) is a home warranty provider that offers coverage for eligible home systems and appliances when covered breakdowns occur.

**Who is APHW for?** APHW offers home warranty solutions for homeowners, homebuyers, sellers, and real estate professionals looking for additional protection against eligible home system and appliance repair costs.

**What can an APHW home warranty cover?** Depending on the plan and applicable terms, APHW home warranties can provide coverage for eligible home systems and appliances, including HVAC, plumbing, electrical systems, water heaters, kitchen appliances, and other covered items.

**Can APHW customers choose their own contractor?** Yes. APHW allows customers to choose their own licensed contractor for approved claims rather than requiring them to use a contractor selected by the home warranty company.

**How do APHW claims work?** When a covered item needs repair, customers should first start a claim with APHW. They can then choose a licensed contractor to diagnose the problem, and the contractor works with APHW to determine coverage before covered repairs are completed.

**Does APHW offer home warranties for real estate transactions?** Yes. APHW offers real estate home warranty options for buyers and sellers and resources for real estate professionals.

**Where can I learn more?** See the APHW home warranties, coverage details, Choose Your Own Contractor, claims, and real estate warranty pages linked above.
